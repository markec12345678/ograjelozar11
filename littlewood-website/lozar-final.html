About Lozar Group

With over 3 years’ experience, we’re an award-winning and trusted partner for cutting-edge security and barrier fencing solutions. Whether you require protection for a commercial facility, industrial site, or residential properties, our range of electronic security and barrier fencing services will be designed to meet your specific needs.

We have a commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction, to deliver the peace of mind you deserve. Discover how our expertise can fortify your infrastructure and provide a holistic approach to the construction phase, from minor civil works through to complex projects.
