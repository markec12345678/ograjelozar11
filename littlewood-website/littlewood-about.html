Our History

The Littlewood story so far...

1996

Company founded:

Littlewood Farm diversifies to become Littlewood Fencing.
2001

Management restructure

Executive team strengthened with key appointments in the next few years with Jon Hobden, Managing Director and Graham Jackson, Technical Director.
2001

HS1 contract awarded

First £1m + contract with the move into large infrastructure projects.
2004

Expansion to new premises

The company purchases Anglian Timber and moves to a new six acre site in Battle, East Sussex.
2007

Security Division established

High-security fencing package awarded with HMP Elmley and HMP Lindholme.
2007

Wales & West expansion

Opened new offices and hired 6 staff in Pontypool, Wales.
2010

Gallagher Channel Partner

The company secures first Channel Partnership.
2010

Awarded Olympic Games contract in London

2015

Coquelles Terminal Calais Awarded

1st overseas project.
2016

Awarded East Sussex Highways Framework

Over 100+ projects completed under this framework.
2017

Behavioural Safety Programme launched

Health and safety initiative launched to the entire business.
2018

HS2 Enabling Works Central awarded

The companies first £10m+ contract.
2018

Embracing the digital frontier

Launched our self-developed internal portal, Littlewood Link.
2019

Health and safety initiatives launched

Major Hand & Arm Vibration (HAVs) initiative and 3 Year Buried Service Strikes Programme launched.
2021

Awarded our first VRS Framework

Working directly for National Highways in areas 1-4 Cornwall to Kent.
2022

HS2 and Align Colne Valley Viaduct awarded

1st £25m + contract for installation of several bespoke noise attenuation systems.
2022

AFI Awards

Awarded Association of Fencing Industries Contactor of the Year £3m+ plus.
2023

Automation subdivision highest value project

Awarded Wates Group Ajax Avenue Data Centre.
2023

AFI Awards

Awarded Association of Fencing Industries Sports and Education Contractor of the Year Award 2023.
2023

iAuditor

Introduction of iAuditor Health and Safety programme
2023

Acquisition of Berry Systems

The group expands by acquiring a market leader company specialising in vehicle parking systems.
2024

Midlands expansion

The Group opened new office in Wolverhampton to service central and northern clients.
2024

£40m turnover

Turnover reaches new high of £40m and 240 directly employed staff.
2024

Acquired by Network Plus

Accelerating Littlewood Group's growth as one of the UK’s leading fencing contractors.
